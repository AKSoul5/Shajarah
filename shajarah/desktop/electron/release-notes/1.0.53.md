## Shajarah 1.0.53: clearer side branches, and a man's wives' boxes by their own children

Poster fixes. Your families, project files and the database are not changed.

- **Side branches grow from their own ancestor's badge.** On a long trunk, each side branch and childless son now leaves from under his own ancestor's badge, in a share of that side's room, so neighbouring families no longer cross each other. On a 340-person family with a long trunk, the lines that crossed, merged or ran under names went from 93 to none, and names at A0 grew from 3.2 mm to 4.6 mm.
- **A childless son's line no longer touches the next badge's son.** In English, on a 15-ancestor trunk at A1, the last such problem is gone, with names the same size.
- **A man's wives' boxes stand by their own children.** With spouses shown, the son with the largest branch is placed where each mother's children stay together, so one wife's lines no longer cross the other's. Across 30 invented two-wife families, 3 posters got clearer and none got worse.

### Verified before this draft was made
- Typecheck, lint, every unit test and the layout QA (448 cases, no new defect).
- Start-up safety tests with the real PostgreSQL 16 on Linux and Windows.
- Upgrades from every earlier release that still has its installer, 1.0.52 included, using a family created by that release and updated through its own updater. The family is identical afterwards.
- Reinstalling 1.0.52 after updating still opens the family.

### Going back
- **The "latest" label on GitHub.** If 1.0.53 is withdrawn, 1.0.52 is marked latest again. That only stops copies that have **not** updated from being offered 1.0.53.
- **Your installation.** You can install 1.0.52 again over 1.0.53, and your families open unchanged. Do not go back to 1.0.51 or older: they bring back the database deletion defect fixed in 1.0.52.
- **Your data.** 1.0.53 changes no data, so nothing needs restoring.

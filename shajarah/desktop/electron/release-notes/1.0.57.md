## Shajarah 1.0.57: cleaner side families

Poster fix. Your families, project files and the database are not changed.

- **More posters come out with no line under a name.** The step that moves a name a little to clear a line from under another gave up on drawings it wrongly thought too tangled: it counted the dotted lines of a daughter's family, leaving her husband along one shared stem, as attached to nothing. It now sees them as they are drawn, and it can also move the names of the trunk's side families. On a 340-person family with its daughters hidden, A2 is now clean; as saved, A2 has one problem fewer.

Layout QA: all 448 posters clean; one has larger names, none worse, and laying out takes no longer.

### Verified before this draft was made
- Typecheck, lint, every unit test and the layout QA (448 cases, no new defect).
- Start-up safety tests with the real PostgreSQL 16 on Linux and Windows.
- Upgrades from every earlier release that still has its installer, using a family created by that release and updated through its own updater. The family is identical afterwards.
- Reinstalling the previous release after updating still opens the family.

### Going back
- **The "latest" label on GitHub.** If 1.0.57 is withdrawn, the previous release is marked latest again. That only stops copies that have **not** updated from being offered 1.0.57.
- **Your installation.** You can install the previous release again over 1.0.57, and your families open unchanged. Do not go back to 1.0.51 or older: they bring back the database deletion defect fixed in 1.0.52.
- **Your data.** 1.0.57 changes no data, so nothing needs restoring.

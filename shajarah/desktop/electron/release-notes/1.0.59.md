## Shajarah 1.0.59: side families apart

A poster fix. Your families, project files and the database are not changed.

- **Side families no longer run into each other.** Where two brothers of your ancestors branch from the trunk close together, their families could fan out on top of each other, with a son of one standing under the lines of the next. When a poster comes out with problems like this, Shajarah now lays it out again with a little room between those families, and keeps that only if it is cleaner and the names are no smaller. On a 340-person family with wives and daughters hidden, the 4A0 poster went from one problem to none and its names grew from 6.6 to 7.7 mm; as saved, A0 went from two problems to one.
- A poster that is already clean is laid out exactly as before.

Layout QA: all 448 posters clean and unchanged.

### Verified before this draft was made
- Typecheck, lint, every unit test and the layout QA (448 cases, no new defect).
- Start-up safety tests with the real PostgreSQL 16 on Linux and Windows.
- Upgrades from every earlier release that still has its installer, using a family created by that release and updated through its own updater. The family is identical afterwards.
- Reinstalling the previous release after updating still opens the family.

### Going back
- **The "latest" label on GitHub.** If 1.0.59 is withdrawn, the previous release is marked latest again. That only stops copies that have **not** updated from being offered 1.0.59.
- **Your installation.** You can install the previous release again over 1.0.59, and your families open unchanged. Do not go back to 1.0.51 or older: they bring back the database deletion defect fixed in 1.0.52.
- **Your data.** 1.0.59 changes no data, so nothing needs restoring.

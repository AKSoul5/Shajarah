## Shajarah 1.0.55: childless sons as ordinary names, and a faster editor

Poster fixes and speed. Your families, project files and the database are not changed.

- **Childless sons beside the trunk are ordinary names.** A trunk ancestor's son with no descendants was drawn as a large ivory medallion, the largest name off the trunk and in the colour the key gives the line of fathers. He is now an ordinary son's name, a little smaller than his brothers who have families. On a crowded invented trunk the smallest names grew by 16% at A1 and 11% at A0; on one design at A1 they are 7% smaller.
- **A faster editor where the centred son's mother stands upright.** Since 1.0.54 such posters are laid out with her upright and with her placed as before, and the better is kept. The editor now makes the two at the same time: on a family that needs both, 3.4 s became 2.6 s. The poster is exactly the same.

Layout QA: all 448 posters clean and unchanged.

### Verified before this draft was made
- Typecheck, lint, every unit test and the layout QA (448 cases, no new defect).
- Start-up safety tests with the real PostgreSQL 16 on Linux and Windows.
- Upgrades from every earlier release that still has its installer, using a family created by that release and updated through its own updater. The family is identical afterwards.
- Reinstalling the previous release after updating still opens the family.

### Going back
- **The "latest" label on GitHub.** If 1.0.55 is withdrawn, the previous release is marked latest again. That only stops copies that have **not** updated from being offered 1.0.55.
- **Your installation.** You can install the previous release again over 1.0.55, and your families open unchanged. Do not go back to 1.0.51 or older: they bring back the database deletion defect fixed in 1.0.52.
- **Your data.** 1.0.55 changes no data, so nothing needs restoring.

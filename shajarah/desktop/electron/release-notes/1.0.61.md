## Shajarah 1.0.61: your poster opens at once

Your families, project files and the database are not changed, apart from a small record of how each family's latest posters were made, kept with the family.

- **A family opens with its poster at once.** Every time the app started, a large family's poster was worked out again from the beginning: about four minutes for a 340-person family at 2A0, with export waiting. The app now keeps, with each family, a short record of how its latest posters were made (one per paper size and style, the 12 most recent). Open the family again and the same poster is back in about a second (1.1 s for the 340-person family on a test computer, ready to export at 1.4 s), with nothing worked out again.
- **After an edit, the poster is back at once too.** Change a name and the poster is made again from that record straight away (in 2 s); the full search runs behind it, as before, and replaces it only if it finds a better one (about 5 minutes for the 340-person family on the same computer). Export waits until the poster is final, as before.
- **The record goes where the family goes.** It is saved inside the family's project file (.shajarah) and comes back when the file is opened, even as a new family. It is not an edit: there is nothing to undo, the family's "last edited" time does not change, and it is left out of GEDCOM.
- **The wait never looks frozen.** The first time a poster is laid out (minutes for a large family), a quick draft of it is on screen within about a second, under a slim strip that says "Draft — finishing the layout…", with how far the layout has got ("Laying out: step 4 · 1:23 elapsed") and, where the family's last layout on this paper took a known time, about how long is left. The finished poster takes its place without moving the view, and "Poster ready" shows for a moment. Until then Export for print, and the Export Poster items in the menu bar, are disabled ("Available when the poster is finished"): the menu bar used to export whatever was on screen.
- **The member list starts at the family.** The ancestors above where the canopy starts are folded into one row at the top ("Ancestral line · 70 people"), so the list opens at the family rather than at آدم; open it with a click, and a search opens it when it finds someone there. Each person's generation now reads from the canopy base, in words ("Son of the canopy base", "3rd generation from فضل", "5 generations above فضل"), with the number from the root kept small beside it. The coloured dots, which meant nothing, are gone.
- **Hidden marriages stay hidden in an opened project file.** A marriage hidden from the poster came back on it when the family's project file was opened as a new family. It now stays hidden.
- **No more crossing at the foot of the canopy.** Where two sons' lines leave the canopy base, a thick limb could set out below a thinner brother's line and sweep across it just past the base: the one problem on a 340-person family's 2A0 poster, which was therefore never clean. Lines now leave the base in the order their names stand round it. That poster is now clean.

Layout QA: all 448 test posters exactly as in 1.0.60 (no new problem, the same name sizes).

### Verified before this draft was made
- Typecheck, lint, every unit test and the layout QA (448 cases, no new defect).
- Start-up safety tests with the real PostgreSQL 16 on Linux and Windows.
- Upgrades from every earlier release that still has its installer, using a family created by that release and updated through its own updater. The family is identical afterwards.
- Reinstalling the previous release after updating still opens the family.

### Going back
- **The "latest" label on GitHub.** If 1.0.61 is withdrawn, the previous release is marked latest again. That only stops copies that have **not** updated from being offered 1.0.61.
- **Your installation.** You can install 1.0.60 again over 1.0.61, and your families open unchanged; 1.0.60 ignores the poster records (each poster is then worked out in full, as before). Do not go back to 1.0.51 or older: they bring back the database deletion defect fixed in 1.0.52.
- **Your data.** 1.0.61 adds only the poster records to each family's settings, so nothing needs restoring.

## Shajarah 1.0.60: a new fan for the canopy

A poster redesign. Your families, project files and the database are not changed.

- **Every generation on one ring.** The canopy is now a fan of rings round the canopy base: each generation's names stand on one oval, the same shape for every generation and the same on both sides. A name never steps off its ring to clear a neighbour; a crowded generation's ring moves further out instead, and only where the page runs out are the names set smaller.
- **Larger names on most posters.** On the 448 test posters, names are larger on 335 and 16% larger on median. On a 340-person family, the A0 poster went from 3.20 to 3.38 mm with no problem (one before), and 2A0 from 3.39 to 5.57 mm.
- **Titles on the second line.** A title typed at the end of a name ("الملقب بالأمين", "ذو النورين", "الأكبر", "الشيخ", "رحمه الله") now sits on the name plate's quiet second line, as a laqab does, so the plate is no wider than the name. The name you entered is unchanged.
- **The centred son rises straight up the middle**, his mother upright above her husband, his brothers on either side.
- **One spouse ring per generation.** A wife or husband with children stands on the way from the partner to the middle of their children; a childless one straight out from the partner.
- **A daughter never heads a branch.** A daughter whose family is shown through her has no badge or colour of her own; her family is drawn like any other branch, on dotted lines.
- **Side families on the trunk** are nested boughs, one fan per side, each family in its own wedge; sons without children are level twigs. Leaves are level: Arabic is never tilted.
- **No empty band inside a drawn frame.** The title and the credit sit close to the frame, and the tree has that room.
- **Neighbouring branches never share a colour.** Each design now has sixteen branch colours, its eight and a shade of each in the same colour family, and every branch takes a colour clearly different from the branches beside it, the trunk's side families included. A poster's first eight branches keep their colours; up to sixteen branches each have their own.
- Laid out faster: the layout QA runs in 31% less time.

Layout QA: no poster with a new problem. Three posters of the 138-person test family with every family shown come out clean at A1 but with names 4 to 6% under the legible size (3.02, 3.07 and 3.08 mm against 3.20); Shajarah recommends A0 for them.

**Known, to be fixed in 1.0.61** (each recorded in the tests, not hidden): on small test families, a few lines under names or crossing among side families on a crowded trunk; one crossing between two wives' lines; two wives' boxes overlapping on a dense 90-person family; and, in four designs, a name at the end of the innermost ring set below the canopy base beside the trunk. Posters with the alternative name-box shapes (scallop, cameo, octagon, shield) come out with smaller names than in 1.0.59.

### Verified before this draft was made
- Typecheck, lint, every unit test and the layout QA (448 cases, no new defect).
- Start-up safety tests with the real PostgreSQL 16 on Linux and Windows.
- Upgrades from every earlier release that still has its installer, using a family created by that release and updated through its own updater. The family is identical afterwards.
- Reinstalling the previous release after updating still opens the family.

### Going back
- **The "latest" label on GitHub.** If 1.0.60 is withdrawn, the previous release is marked latest again. That only stops copies that have **not** updated from being offered 1.0.60.
- **Your installation.** You can install the previous release again over 1.0.60, and your families open unchanged. Do not go back to 1.0.51 or older: they bring back the database deletion defect fixed in 1.0.52.
- **Your data.** 1.0.60 changes no data, so nothing needs restoring.

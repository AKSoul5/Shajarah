## Shajarah 1.0.54: the centred son's mother stands upright, and smoother limbs

Poster fixes. Your families, project files and the database are not changed.

- **The centred son's mother stands straight above her husband.** With spouses shown, the wife at the base of the canopy who bore the son with the largest branch was set towards the middle of her children, so when most of them were on one side she leaned off the trunk, pressed against her husband's name. She now stands straight above him, and her line to him and her son's limb from her rise straight up. On a 340-person family at A0 she had leaned 33° off the trunk. Where standing her upright would leave the names smaller, she is placed as before.
- **Limbs grow gradually from behind each name.** A thick limb used to start at its full width with a round joint, which drew a disc wider than the name around it (most visible on the centred branch), and a wife's line from the base of the canopy flared out of the trunk beside her husband's name. Every limb is now no wider than the names it joins and widens smoothly, so no joint shows.
- **The trunk rises into the canopy base's oval.** Its rounded top used to stand out around the oval as a hump; it is now hidden behind it.

Layout QA: all 448 posters clean; 3 have larger names, none smaller.

### Verified before this draft was made
- Typecheck, lint, every unit test and the layout QA (448 cases, no new defect).
- Start-up safety tests with the real PostgreSQL 16 on Linux and Windows.
- Upgrades from every earlier release that still has its installer, 1.0.53 included, using a family created by that release and updated through its own updater. The family is identical afterwards.
- Reinstalling 1.0.53 after updating still opens the family.

### Going back
- **The "latest" label on GitHub.** If 1.0.54 is withdrawn, 1.0.53 is marked latest again. That only stops copies that have **not** updated from being offered 1.0.54.
- **Your installation.** You can install 1.0.53 again over 1.0.54, and your families open unchanged. Do not go back to 1.0.51 or older: they bring back the database deletion defect fixed in 1.0.52.
- **Your data.** 1.0.54 changes no data, so nothing needs restoring.
